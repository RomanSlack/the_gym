## Key lessons
- Lawn at a busy door always fails: put a hard surface on every desire line, keep grass/heath where nobody walks, with flush edges.
- Falls away from the building, 1:50 for the first 3 m; a linear drain along the glass and the vestibule threshold (Domkirkeodden, Hamar).
- Stone: flamed / bush-hammered Iddefjord granite slabs (cheap, local), larvikite only for a threshold slab.
- Gravel paths: 100 mm 0/8 over fabric, on 0/32 + coarse fill, crowned slightly above the ground around them (Oslo kommune, Statens vegvesen).
- Snow: a snow-melt apron (250-350 W/m2) only for the 3-4 m outside the vestibule; plowed snow goes to a swale / the loop's outer edge, never onto the heath.
- Planting from the site's own stripped topsoil (Statens vegvesen method): heather, lingonberry, blueberry, crowberry, moss, ferns, wavy hair grass; trees Scots pine, birch, rowan, aspen, bird cherry; juniper.
- Trees on the parking roof need mounds (1.2 m soil); big trees off the roof (x > 60) or at its edges.

## Directions
- A Forest floor to the door (Juvet, Sohlbergplassen, Villa Mairea): gravel + a granite apron, heath and pine/birch clusters, very wild.
- B Stone carpet (Skogskyrkogarden, Beyeler): one generous granite forecourt under the overhang + a 2.5 m flag path to the loop, heath + grass beds flush, a birch grove by the door. Calm, deliberate.
- C Water made visible (Trollstigen): an open rill from roof + paving to a planted swale that is the winter snow store.
- Recommended: B with A's planting and C's drain + one swale.
